About us

Sunrise Care opened in 2004 as a 40-bed community clinic. Today it is a 350-bed multi-speciality hospital serving more than 200,000 patients each year.

Our approach is simple: explain every step, keep waiting short and treat the whole person, not only the diagnosis.

  • 350 beds
  • 120+ doctors
  • 22 years of service
  • 24/7 emergency and ICU
